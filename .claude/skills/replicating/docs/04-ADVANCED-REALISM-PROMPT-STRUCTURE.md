# CLAUDE INSTRUCTION — PHOTO REALISM (LEVEL 3, LOCKED)

You help generate ad/UGC images that look like a REAL casual phone photo.
Apply these rules to EVERY image that should look like a real photo (UGC, lifestyle,
portraits, react/selfie/story shots, "make it look real"). Do NOT apply to clean
studio PRODUCT packshots (white background), which have their own look.
Write the final image prompt in English. Generate ONE clean image (no "level"
comparisons). Engine: gpt_image_2_5 / GPT-Image, resolution 1k, aspect 9:16 or 3:4.

## GOAL
Make it look like an ordinary photo a real person snapped on their phone — NOT
"beautiful", NOT "cinematic", NOT "high quality". Realism comes from the LIGHTING,
subtle SENSOR artifacts and natural SKIN TEXTURE — NEVER from making the person ugly,
plain or tired. The person stays natural and normal (attractive-but-real is fine),
just not an airbrushed glossy model.

## HOW TO USE
Write [WHO + WHERE + WHAT THEY'RE DOING + FRAMING], then append the REALISM BLOCK
below unchanged.

## REALISM BLOCK — paste into every prompt
> casual realistic ordinary iPhone photo, NOT hyper-realistic, flat everyday
> phone-camera look, faint sensor grain, mild JPEG compression, candid slightly
> off-center and slightly tilted framing. NEUTRAL natural white balance (not
> warm/orange graded, not saturated). Everything in deep focus — NO background blur,
> NO bokeh, NO depth-of-field. NOT cinematic, NOT color-graded, NOT HDR, NOT studio,
> NOT dramatic, NOT polished.
>
> LIGHTING: one dominant DIRECTIONAL light with a clear origin and a shadow side (a
> window to one side, the far side in gentle shadow); the window/bright source is
> slightly OVER-EXPOSED and blown-out white; a warm lamp adds a SUBTLE local warm/cool
> cast near it (not a global cast). Slightly crushed shadows, one small blown
> highlight, faint lens haze.
>
> SENSOR (keep it SUBTLE): a gentle Smart-HDR look, shadows a touch lifted; faint
> grain; mild compression. NOT over-sharpened, NO crunchy halos, NO noise-reduction
> smear, NO green/teal shadow tint, NO global blue/cold wash. Color stays
> neutral-to-slightly-warm, never bluish or grey.
>
> SKIN: real natural skin with visible pores, slight oil sheen, a small blemish,
> faint under-eye shadow, flyaway hairs, subtle facial asymmetry — NO AI-glossy skin.
> The person is normal and natural-looking (attractive-but-real), NEVER deliberately
> plain, tired or unattractive.
>
> A calm, settled, natural expression, eyes fully open, NOT mid-blink, NOT caught in
> motion, NO motion blur — everything crisp and frozen (so it can be animated after).
> iPhone 1x lens, no fish-eye. NO added text, captions, watermark or logo.

## SCENE LIGHTING SWAPS
- Indoor store/office: swap the window line for "bright overhead FLUORESCENT, slight
  greenish-cool cast, one small blown highlight, no window" (keep overall color neutral).
- Outdoor daytime: "flat bright overcast or hard midday sun, blown white sky, no
  golden hour, no warm grade."

## NEVER USE THESE WORDS
beautiful, cinematic, professional, studio, dramatic, moody, color-graded, HDR,
bokeh, shallow depth of field, golden hour, award-winning, masterpiece, highly
detailed, hyperrealistic, 8k, 4k, RAW photo, film grain, 35mm, "shot on iPhone Pro".
